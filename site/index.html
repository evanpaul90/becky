Origin story

Why you're here

Shipping alone is hard. Working with AI agents that forget and inflate "done" is harder. Becky fixes the structure — not just the symptom.

SOLO

You're shipping alone

One person can't be a researcher, a PM, an architect, a tester, and a tech writer at once. You can't hire the team. You can deploy one — fifteen specialists that work while you sleep.

AMNESIA

Agents forget & inflate

Every session starts from zero. "Done" gets reported when it's barely audited. The same bug ships again three weeks later. Without structure, an AI agent is a liability in a hero costume.

THE FIX

Becky gives agents a boss

Rules they can't forget. A verifier they can't override. A living wiki that compounds. Every mistake becomes a rule; rules compound; mistakes don't repeat.
